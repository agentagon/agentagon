# Explain a measured baseline

Input: completed controlled measurement, frozen evaluator, accepted goal and optional recent-trace score.

Explain each component in its original units, required behavior outcomes, composite scoring and missing evidence. Keep production samples separate from the controlled benchmark. Show dataset/evaluator/source identities and limits on comparison. A preparation trial is not a substitute for the optimization baseline.

Output: a concise baseline explanation and evidence-backed optimization context. The engine owns numeric calculation and saved measurements. New evidence or revised scoring creates a new version; never rewrite a frozen baseline to improve its appearance.
